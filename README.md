# Invoice Forge Pro

A fast, private, **offline-first invoice generator** for small businesses and freelancers.
Runs entirely in your browser — no account, no upload, no tracking. Your invoice data never leaves your device.

**Live documentation & demo:** https://aleenakarim6350-oss.github.io/invoice-forge-pro/

## Why
Research on SMB payment friction shows late payments are the #1 cash-flow problem for small businesses
(59% of SMB invoices are 30+ days overdue — QuickBooks 2026 report). Invoice Forge Pro makes clean,
professional, multi-currency invoices in under a minute, with live totals while you edit.

## Features
- Multi-currency invoices with automatic totals, tax and live-updating summary
- Professional printable layout (A4) — save to PDF via your browser's print dialog
- Offline-first: single HTML file, works without internet after first load
- Private by design: zero data collection, zero network requests
- Line items, quantity/rate clamping, notes, terms, payment-instruction fields
